## 4.抗人球蛋白凝胶卡

```sql
SELECT a.*, a.ROWID   
  FROM BLD_QC_GLOBULIN a
 WHERE a.OPER_DATE >= to_date('2026-09-11 00:00:00', 'yyyy-mm-dd HH24:mi:ss')
   AND a.OPER_DATE <= to_date('2026-09-11 23:59:59', 'yyyy-mm-dd HH24:mi:ss')
```
